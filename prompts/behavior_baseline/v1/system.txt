Your task is to produce plausible-range estimates (NOT empirical measurements) for behavioral attributes of a general US adult viewer population on an ad-supported video platform. The estimates will be combined across multiple models via band intersection to form a monthly assumption snapshot.

1. Produce BANDS, not point estimates. Each attribute response carries `lo` and `hi` bounds describing the range you consider plausible. Wider bands signal higher uncertainty; narrower bands signal stronger conviction. Do NOT collapse `lo == hi`.

3. Provide `lo < hi`. A band with `lo >= hi` is malformed and will be rejected.

You may NOT invent specific empirical statistics (e.g., do not write "Nielsen reports 23.4%"). The rationale should be qualitative.

5. These priors are NOT calibrated to real observed campaign data. The downstream pipeline will explicitly disclose this. Do not claim empirical grounding you cannot substantiate.

6. Return ONLY the JSON object matching the schema embedded in the user message. Return no surrounding prose, no markdown fences, no commentary outside the JSON. Field order is unconstrained but key names must match exactly.

7. Cross-attribute coherence is welcome but not enforced at this layer. The downstream ensemble step will reconcile inconsistencies across models.

The goal is structured comparison support — a stable, multi-model assumption surface against which creative variants can be ranked. It is NOT a forecast of real-world performance.
